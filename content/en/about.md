## How I think about security

**Process over payloads.**  
Recon → enumeration → exploit → post-exploitation → report. You can have a thousand tools and still get nowhere without method. Tools change. Thinking stays.

**Blue Team builds the foundation for Red Team.**  
Before you start attacking, you need to understand what you are protecting and why. Defence explains attack paths faster than any “ethical hacking from zero” course ever will.

**Less magic, more understanding.**  
Copying commands without knowing why they work leads nowhere. Here, every command has context - what it does, how the mechanism behind it works, and where it may fail.

## Who this manual is for

For people who:

- are learning offensive or defensive security and want **one proper place** for notes instead of 40 browser tabs
- do CTFs, labs, and TryHackMe, and prefer **field notes** over a textbook written by committee
- want to understand tools - not just launch them
- are just getting started and want someone who explains things like a colleague, not like a lecturer

You can also **download the notes offline** - each one as a PDF or raw Markdown. The Export button is in the top-right corner. Use them without internet access if you need to.
